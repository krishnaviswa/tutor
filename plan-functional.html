Functional map

What the product does

TutorOS is subject-neutral. Templates are arrangements of seven jobs, not subject SKUs. Biology/NEET/NCERT is one exam-prep example tenant.

004 wired all 47 screens. 005 (Specified) lifts demo named facts onto those screens — see density-map.html. The table below is the 003 historical empty/planned snapshot; live status is catalog/screens.json.
  1. Let people in. router, student-login, staff-login, wsetup, roster, cohort-builder, parent-link
  2. Schedule and deliver. schedule, session-pre, join, live-teacher, live-student
  3. Practice. qbank, practice-build, practice-play, practice-result, test-setup, test-runner, analysis
  4. Doubts. doubt-student, doubt-teacher
  5. Record. record, session-video, timeline
  6. Progress. student-dash, teacher-dash, owner, reports, mentor
  7. Money. billing, payments, subscription, payouts

Roles

Owner

Workspace, branding, quotas, billing, templates, integrations. Screens under /app/admin.

Teacher

Cohorts, schedule, live, record, practice, doubts, announce. Exam-prep starts at cohort/schedule — not staff-login.

Assistant

Roster rights. Not owner consoles. Not required to PATCH record.

Student

Join, live, library, practice, tests, own timeline, own dashboard, doubts, payments.

Parent

parent-home is a slice of the linked child: timeline, reports, practice-result, payments, messages, notif-prefs.

Templates (named toggle sets)

exam-prep, 1-on-1, K-12, skills, music, everything. Exam-prep omits staff-login. Others include it. Same 47 ids everywhere.
