# Video links & transcripts — Google Meet + Microsoft Teams

**Decision for `TutorOS`: two providers only — Google Meet and Microsoft Teams.**
Zoom, Jitsi, YouTube-Live and everything else are out of scope: the platform will not create
links for them and will not import their transcripts. A tenant on one of those pastes a plain
link and loses automatic attendance and transcript — that is the deliberate cost of being
off-platform.

**The platform never hosts video.** Every session runs on Meet or Teams. The platform's job is
(a) put the right join link in front of students and (b) pull the record — attendance, and later
the transcript — back afterwards. This keeps video at **$0** to run (blueprint §7.3). Hosting
rooms or running speech-to-text ourselves both break that, so both stay out of v1 and the
prototype.

Companion screen: **Session video & transcript** in `tutor-platform-demo.html` (Teach stage). Two
states — *generated Meet link* (default) and *use a Microsoft Teams link* — plus a transcript
panel that stays empty until a provider file is imported.

---

## The model

| Mode | How the link is made | Marginal cost | Recording / transcript owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Generated Meet (default)** | Platform creates a Google Meet link on the tenant's own Google account (Calendar API `conferenceData`) at schedule time | $0 | Tenant's Google Drive |
| **Microsoft Teams** | Tenant connects Microsoft 365 (Graph `onlineMeetings` create) or pastes a Teams meeting link | $0 | Tenant's OneDrive / SharePoint |
| **Hosted rooms** | Platform-run rooms (LiveKit / Daily / 100ms) | ≈ $0.004–0.006 / participant-minute + storage + our own STT | Platform — **not built**, revisit only as a metered add-on for tenants on neither Google nor Microsoft |

Default is **Generated Meet**: free, the tenant already trusts Google, calendar entries come for
free, and join/leave events flow back so attendance is automatic. **Microsoft Teams** is the
fallback for institutes standardised on M365 — same $0, slightly more setup, transcript import
lands second on the build order.

## Provider comparison

### 1. Link generation

| Provider | Programmatic create | API surface | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Meet** | Yes | Google Calendar API — add `conferenceData` to an event on the tenant's calendar | One OAuth consent covers Calendar + Drive. Default path, built first. |
| **Microsoft Teams** | Yes | Microsoft Graph `POST /me/onlineMeetings` (or `/users/{id}/onlineMeetings`), Azure AD app | v1 = the tenant pastes a Teams link; Graph create is a fast-follow. |

### 2. Transcript & recording retrieval

| Provider | Transcript | How we pull it | Plan gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Meet** | Meet "Transcripts" (and Gemini "take notes for me") write a Google Doc; recordings write an MP4 | Drive API: watch the tenant's *Meet Recordings* folder and fetch the Doc / MP4, or the tenant pastes the Doc link | Transcripts / recording need an eligible Google Workspace edition (Business Standard+ / Education); a free `@gmail.com` account gets neither automatically |
| **Microsoft Teams** | Meeting transcript (`.vtt`) and recording (in OneDrive / SharePoint) | Graph `GET /me/onlineMeetings/{id}/transcripts` then `.../content`; recording via the drive item | Transcription requires an M365 plan that includes it; org policy can disable it |
| **Either, manual** | — | Tenant opens the provider's transcript, copies the text, pastes it into the session record | Always available — the universal fallback |

### 3. Attendance

| Provider | Automatic attendance |
|---|---|
| **Google Meet (generated by us)** | Yes — participant events come back through the connected Google account; reinforced by our own join page |
| **Microsoft Teams (Graph, later)** | Partial — attendance reports exist via Graph on eligible plans; until then, platform join-page tracking only |
| **Any pasted link** | Platform-side only — we count who entered the call from our join page |

## What the prototype screen shows

- **State A — Generated Meet (default).** Meet link, "created from `anitha@meridianbio.in` · no
  cost", auto-attendance badge, "Regenerate" / "Copy".
- **State B — Microsoft Teams.** Paste field for a Teams meeting link (Graph connect later), an
  embed preview, and the caveat: *"attendance for a pasted link is platform join-tracking only."*
- **Transcript panel.** `transcript_segments` — *empty*. Actions: **Import from Google Drive**,
  **Import from Microsoft 365**, **Paste transcript**, and a disabled **Request speech-to-text
  (add-on)** naming the ≈ $0.04 / audio-minute cost. This matches the `transcript_segments` seam
  in blueprint §3.2 — a future STT job fills the same slot without a schema change.
- The UI word is **"session record"**, never "transcript", until real STT ships (blueprint §11.3).

---

## Recommendation

1. **Support Google Meet + Microsoft Teams only.** No Zoom, no others; no hosted rooms; no STT
   in v1 or the prototype.
2. **Build order:** generated Meet → paste-a-Teams-link → auto-import from Google Drive →
   Graph transcript import for Teams.
3. **Set the expectation in onboarding:** "We don't host the video — we put your Meet or Teams
   link in the student app and keep the record. Verbatim transcripts come from Google or
   Microsoft."
4. **Keep hosted rooms on the roadmap** as a metered add-on for the minority on neither Google
   nor Microsoft — priced at cost + margin, never bundled.
