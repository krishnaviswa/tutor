Highlights in this release

  • 1-Click Workspace & Demo Persona Picker: Every sign-in gate includes Demo Persona Quick-Select and Workspace dropdowns, and the router screen (/app/student/router) includes a direct Workspace Owner / Admin door alongside Student, Teacher, and Parent.
  • Horizontal Section Sub-Tabs: Multi-screen workflows display horizontal section sub-tabs across Schedule, Students, Practice & Assessments (steps 1–6), Doubts & Comms, Records & Reports, Owner Console, Student Classes/Practice/You, and Parent Home/Reports.
  • Guided Forms & Consequence Previews: Structured <select> dropdowns, collapsible + New / + Add creation forms, Rupee (₹) billing presets and inputs, and the 3-step Attendance & Class Summary flow (/app/faculty/record) with live lesson-pack consequence previews before saving.

What this pass shipped, and what it left out, is in build-plan.md. The guides cover the 8-hour demo pack (480 minutes on balance_minutes and purchased_minutes, only after a seed that includes seed_internal_v2; balance on parent home and on payments), the 24-hour burn rule (60 minutes when a session has no end), Schedule saying Hour stays or Hour is used before save, four-way student attendance (a stored present still reads as Attended, and absent as No-show), payouts of delivered sessions (delivered and hours), the owner enquiry (name, phone, hours), and a parent booking an open hour.
