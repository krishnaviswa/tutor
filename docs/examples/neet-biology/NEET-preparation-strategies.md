# NEET Biology — Preparation Strategies and Implementation Plan (Tutor)

Source: strategies and plan as discussed in this tutoring conversation. Not rewritten as new advice. Official reference: **NMC/NTA NEET (UG) 2026 syllabus** (updated December 2025). NEET Biology does not map perfectly 1:1 to NCERT chapter names; the topic sequence below is a practical teaching sequence based on Class 11 and Class 12 NCERT, with NEET preparation in mind.

**Context for this plan**

- Goal: prepare **yourself first**, then create a master NEET Biology roadmap, then produce teaching material systematically — before launching every topic.
- Constraint: a polished PPT can take **2–3 days** per topic (TCA PPT took 3 days).
- Channel: YouTube + online classes + long-term coaching brand.
- Current work: Plant Physiology / **Respiration in Plants** is the current major project (TCA). **Body Fluids & Circulation** is an existing topic. Well into respiration (#8 in the first phase model). Circulation already done (#12 in that model). Glycolysis and TCA/Krebs Cycle marked done; ETS/Oxidative Phosphorylation in progress.

## 1. Core strategies (do not skip)

1. **Create the master NEET Biology roadmap first.** Then prepare yourself systematically before worrying about launching every topic.
2. **Do not start making PPTs randomly** from the full topic list.
3. **Do not start from Chapter 1 and blindly go to Chapter 2.**
4. **Do not treat every Biology topic equally.** Goal is to prepare yourself first.
5. **Distinguish A vs B before building every PPT:**
   - **A.** Topics you need to **MASTER for yourself**
   - **B.** Topics you need to **CREATE PPTs for**
   - Those are not the same thing.
6. You do **not** need a 25-slide beautiful presentation for every tiny subtopic. Example: TCA deserves a detailed visual PPT; Taxonomic Aids may not need the same level of production. This distinction could save hundreds of hours.
7. Use **three priority levels** because a polished PPT can take 2–3 days per topic:
   - **Priority 1:** Must master + detailed PPT + strong YouTube potential
   - **Priority 2:** Must master + PPT, but can be less elaborate
   - **Priority 3:** Master NCERT thoroughly; simple PPT/combined lecture is enough
8. Spend the **best PPT-making effort** on Priority 1 topics.
9. Priority 2: important, but **do not spend three days making a cinematic presentation** for each.
10. Priority 3: still **master**; can **combine** or use simpler visual notes. Official syllabus includes these areas — **do not skip them**. They do not need the same 3-day PPT treatment as TCA or Neural Control.
11. **Animal Kingdom** deserves more attention than its YouTube potential suggests because it is highly memory/NCERT dependent and can generate lots of NEET questions.
12. For Priority 1 topics, **do not think “3 days = one PPT.”** Think: **one 3-day topic = a CONTENT PACKAGE.** Example (TCA Cycle): 3 days produce **8–10 usable assets**, not just one PPT:
    - 1 long YouTube video — *TCA Cycle/Krebs Cycle Explained for NEET*
    - 3 Shorts — Why is TCA amphibolic? / How many NADH are produced? / Where is CO₂ released?
    - 1 PYQ video — *5 NEET Questions from TCA*
    - 1 revision video — *TCA Cycle in 5 Minutes*
    - 1 class PPT
    - 1 student worksheet
    - 1 chapter test
13. **Immediate preparation:** for the next few weeks, **do not touch the whole syllabus.**
14. Use the **NMC current NEET UG 2026 syllabus as the master checklist**, rather than relying solely on older NCERT chapter lists.
15. Class 12 highest-value topics: **eventually invest a lot of teaching effort** there. Genetics: **spend a lot of preparation time.** Molecular biology could become one of the strongest YouTube categories. Human Physiology can be an entire YouTube series: **NEET Human Physiology — Complete Series.** After Plant Physiology items 4–8, you have an excellent Plant Physiology YouTube playlist. Genetics gives enormous scope for YouTube **problem-solving** videos because students actively look for help with genetics questions.
